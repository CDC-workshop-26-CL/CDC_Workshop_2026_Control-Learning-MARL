About the workshop

Learning and decision-making for interactive autonomous systems

Autonomous systems increasingly operate in environments shaped by uncertainty, interaction, and strategic behavior. Their successful deployment requires combining tools from control, optimization, machine learning, reinforcement learning, and game theory.

This workshop brings together researchers working at the intersection of these areas to discuss theoretical advances, computational methods, and emerging applications. Particular emphasis is placed on methods that connect rigorous analysis with scalable learning and control architectures for complex dynamical and multi-agent systems.
